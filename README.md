Navidrome Custom Edition
A personal fork of Navidrome with enhanced album organization, playback continuity, and Synology Audio Station integration.

Custom Features
- Album Collections: Create custom album groups, including albums from nested folders.
- Resume Playback: Remember playback progress for albums and playlists.
- Recently Played: Browse previously played albums and playlists with cover artwork.
- M3U Synchronization: Two-way synchronization with Synology Audio Station playlists.
- Scheduled Sync: Automatically synchronize M3U playlists at a configurable time.
- Enhanced Collection Views: Cover/list layouts and Chinese pinyin sorting.
- Mobile Playback Improvements: Custom player behavior for iPad and iPhone.
